# Original UCLA assets

The site uses the original UCLA Mathematics department signature files, without rebuilding the wordmark in HTML or modifying the logo artwork.

- White Mathematics signature: https://ww3.math.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Uxd_Wht_Mathematics_A.png
- Black Mathematics signature: https://ww3.math.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Uxd_Blk_Mathematics_A.png
- Official favicon: https://ww3.math.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/cropped-UCLA-only-512px_White_UCLA-Mathematics-Square-192x192.png
- Mathematical Sciences Building photograph: https://ww3.math.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Math-Sciences-Building-13.jpg
